About

I build data pipelines: large Spark jobs in Scala, Python services and tooling, and SQL models in dbt, orchestrated with Airflow on GCP.

Most of my work is making pipelines correct, cheaper to run and easier to change, including moving legacy systems onto newer ones without breaking what depends on them.

I came into data management through modeling neurons during a master's in Biomedical Sciences with a focus in neuroscience. Outside work I build iOS apps for my family.
