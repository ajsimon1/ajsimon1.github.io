About

I came to engineering through Python and a master's in Biomedical Sciences with a focus in neuroscience, and stayed for the part where a messy problem turns into something that runs every day without anyone thinking about it.

These days that's data engineering: dbt, BigQuery, Airflow, and a lot of questions about why a number changed. I care about pipelines that are correct first and cheap second, and about leaving things easier for whoever comes next.

Outside work I build SwiftUI apps for problems my family actually has, like who's bringing which gift and whose book is on whose shelf.
