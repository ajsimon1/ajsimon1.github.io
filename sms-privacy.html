Stocks SMS Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Operated by Adam Simon, sole proprietor

Stocks SMS is an on-demand stock analysis information service operated by Adam Simon, sole proprietor. Approved subscribers text a stock ticker to the service number and receive one automated informational reply with a rules-based analysis of that stock. This policy explains what information the service handles and how.

Information collected

When you text the service, it receives your mobile phone number and the content of your message. Nothing else is collected. There are no accounts, no website sign-ups and no tracking.

How it is used

Your phone number is used only to confirm you are an approved subscriber and to send the reply to your message. Message content is used only to determine which stock to analyze.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Phone numbers and messages are never sold, rented or shared, except with the service providers that deliver the messages and run the service (Twilio for messaging and Amazon Web Services for hosting), solely to operate it.

Retention

Message records are kept in the providers’ standard logs only as long as needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency depends on how often you text the service; it never sends unsolicited messages. Message and data rates may apply.
