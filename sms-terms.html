Stocks SMS Terms of Service

Effective October 3, 2026 · Operated by Adam Simon, sole proprietor

The service

Stocks SMS is an on-demand stock analysis information service operated by Adam Simon, sole proprietor, and available to a small number of approved subscribers. You text a stock ticker (for example, NFLX) to the service number and receive one automated informational reply summarizing a rules-based analysis of that stock, including its price, business-quality score, trend and analyst price targets.

Consent and frequency

You opt in by texting the service number yourself. The service only replies to messages you send and never initiates contact or sends marketing. Message frequency varies with how often you text it. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to opt out; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for instructions, or email adamjsimon1@yahoo.com.

Not financial advice

Replies are automated, educational output from personal rules applied to public market data. They are not financial, investment or trading advice, and may be delayed or inaccurate. Make your own decisions.
