Stocks SMS: How to Join

On-demand stock analysis by text · Operated by Adam Simon, sole proprietor

Stocks SMS is an on-demand stock analysis information service. Subscribers text a stock ticker (for example, NFLX) to the Stocks SMS number and receive one automated text reply with an informational analysis of that stock: latest price, a business-quality score, the price trend and the average analyst price target. Stocks SMS never texts anyone first and sends no marketing.

Step 1: Request access

Email adamjsimon1@yahoo.com with the subject “Stocks SMS access” and include the mobile number you want to use. Access is limited to a small number of approved subscribers.

Step 2: Give consent

Adam Simon calls you or speaks with you in person and reads this script word for word:

“Stocks SMS, operated by Adam Simon, is an on-demand stock analysis information service. If you agree, you can text a stock ticker symbol to the Stocks SMS number and you will receive one automated text message reply with an informational analysis of that stock. You will only receive messages in response to tickers you send; message frequency varies based on how often you text. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP at any time to opt out. Our terms and privacy policy are at ajsimon1.github.io/sms-terms.html and ajsimon1.github.io/sms-privacy.html. Do you agree to receive these text messages from Stocks SMS?”

Only after you clearly answer yes is your number added to the approved subscriber list. Adam then confirms your enrollment and gives you the Stocks SMS number.

Step 3: Text a ticker

Text a ticker to the Stocks SMS number to get your first reply. Messages from numbers that have not consented are never answered.
